Is it really free? What's the catch?
▼
There is no catch. No subscription, no trial, no ads, no telemetry, no data collection. We built GreenBox because everyone deserves privacy — not just people who can afford enterprise software. Use it personally, commercially, forever. The only thing we ask is nothing.
What happens if I forget my password?
▼
Your file is gone. And we mean that in the best possible way. No backdoor, no recovery mechanism. Your password is the key. No key = no access. This is by design — a backdoor for you is a backdoor for attackers too.
Can hackers break the encryption?
▼
Mathematically, no. AES-256 has never been broken. The best known attack is brute force, which would take longer than the age of the universe with all computing power on Earth. PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations makes password guessing infeasible. Your encrypted file is safe anywhere.
Does GreenBox phone home?
▼
Absolutely not. Zero network connections. Ever. No analytics, no update checker, no crash reporting. Your files, your password, your machine. Works perfectly on air-gapped systems with no network.
